What each page demonstrates

  • Badge: trust score 0–100 (= 100 × trust, trust = ½ − r from the propagation). Hover or click for the explanation.
  • Popover: profile facts, the exact decomposition of the score (prior + one row per network channel, rows sum to the score), the three most influential neighbours with their effect, flags (creation burst, dense block, seed), and the "How does this work?" explainer.
  • Summary card in the side column: how many accounts on the page fall in each band, and which coordinated block is active on the page.
  • Overlay switch in the top bar: hides everything the add-on injects so you can compare with the bare page.
  • Guide with four numbered examples per page and "Show me" buttons that scroll to the example and open its popover.

Files

  • reddit.html, twitter.html: the two pages (plain HTML, no build step, open directly in a browser).
  • overlay.css, overlay.js: the shared add-on pieces (badge, ring, popover, banner, guide, switch). Each page passes its own account table to TrustOverlay.init().
